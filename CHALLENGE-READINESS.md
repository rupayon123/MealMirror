## What the current app can do

| Capability | Current behavior and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Start a meal | First launch asks the person to choose their own meal photo and opens Apple's native library picker; it no longer preselects a Practice meal. Home places Camera and Library above the large theme artwork, with a general Start action. The person can add up to three food views and a separate nutrition-label photo, remove attachments, type a description, or select one of three optional Practice meals. A selected food photo alone can advance to review. Camera capture and return have not been verified on physical hardware. |
| Inspect photos offline | Uses Apple's local Vision classifier on the meal views and a small bundled food catalog. It never uploads images. Possible food matches start excluded, and photos never set portions or carbohydrate grams. A separately selected label photo is transcribed locally with Vision and shown beside the image; OCR never fills an amount automatically. Photo preparation handles 2× and CIImage-backed camera input without retaining an unprepared full-size result; a synthetic large CIImage still had high peak decode memory. The current iOS 26.5 Simulator returned unrelated scene labels for three different meal images; the app shows inspection unavailable and no range in that case. Broad or reliable any-meal recognition is **not implemented**. |
| Recover from an unknown photo | Allows the person to name foods or enter a carbohydrate amount copied from packaging or another trusted source. The result identifies that input as user-entered. A manually entered amount stays fixed when catalog portion settings change and has a direct edit action. This is not nutrition validation. |
| Review an estimate | Shows included and excluded items, adjustable portion choices for local catalog examples, an illustrative range where supported, a source/review trail, and a final confirmation step. An exact manually entered amount is shown as one value and marked as the person's entry. A label photo offers a direct checked-amount entry route. The local catalog is finite and its example ranges are not clinically validated. |
| Learn with Practice | Includes synthetic biryani, grain-bowl, and breakfast images with example review data. These values teach the controls; they are not measurements of those pictured servings. |
| Save and control history | Can save reviewed meal items, their sources and portions, the meal origin (Practice, description, or selected photos), reference-item choice, range, and date in protected local storage; shows saved reviews and offers deletion. Practice is labeled at final Review before Save and in history, where its ranges are explicitly examples. Older records with no saved origin are marked unknown; photo-only records receive a neutral title. Fixed user-entered amounts retain their source and do not acquire a portion label in history. It does not save meal or label photos or OCR text. The exact `ab082da8` ZIP passed the two-view + label → local OCR → manually entered 37 g → save → History → detail → delete journey on iPhone SE Simulator. |
| Work without an account | Meal review, reference content, assets, and storage are local. There is no required server, sign-in, judge tracking, or analytics in the candidate source. Actual offline airplane-mode use on a physical device is still unverified. |
| Present the Pixel Kitchen theme | Uses the selected circular pot emblem with star accents, warm adaptive colors, bundled Pixelify and Nunito fonts, a six-food hand-plotted pixel loading ring, and matching action icons through Home, meal entry, review, and history. Hand-plotted food-view, nutrition-label, add-photo, remove-photo, and Review status icons keep the multi-photo tray and saved state in the same theme. Foods remain upright during the orbit. Compact welcome shows its photo promise and insulin boundary before scrolling; compact Home keeps Camera/Photos prominent and the settings control aligned in Arabic RTL. The updated tray shows food and label slot counts outside its horizontal media strip, numbers each food photo, and switches to a grid at accessibility text sizes. The exact ZIP's home screens were visually inspected on iPhone 17, iPhone SE, and iPad mini. The iPhone SE photo/label flow passed; the iPhone 17 full flow remains unverified because the system Photos picker grid was not exposed to XCTest's expected identifier. Earlier light/dark phone and iPad checks do not cover every new screen. |
| Support languages | The everyday source offers English plus 17 other language choices, with 478 keys in each of the 18 catalogs. The updated carbohydrate-only trusted-source note is translated in all 18 catalogs; the Challenge ZIP contains English only, matching the last published rules. The static audit still finds 34 values identical to English across every non-English locale (including numerals and brand/name entries), plus other locale-specific English-identical values. An older-record sentence and some Privacy text remain uncatalogued. Native-speaker review is open; do not describe everyday localization as complete. |
| Accessibility | Source includes VoiceOver labels/identifiers, section-heading traits, and Reduce Motion handling. Earlier English and Arabic iPhone SE maximum Dynamic Type checks showed the welcome Photo action visible and tappable while safety copy scrolled; Urdu welcome art was also inspected. The new food/label tray was visually inspected at standard size on iPhone SE with no clipping in the captured frames. A source run passed the dark accessibility XXXL description → manual amount → Estimate path. The preceding `283eb669` ZIP's extracted app passed a dark iPad mini description → manual amount → Estimate path, and its screenshot showed the title, amount source, safety text, and bottom action without clipping; it also passed the iPhone SE photo/label journey. The current `3d527eed` ZIP was visually checked at standard size on iPhone 17 and iPhone SE home screens. Earlier iPad mini light onboarding, picker, Practice estimate/Review, and History were inspected on older bytes. The accessibility-sized photo tray has not yet been visually captured end-to-end because the native Photos notice interrupted that attempt. Reduce Motion was toggled in a Simulator on an earlier ZIP; animation suppression on the current ZIP remains unverified. Direct VoiceOver speech/swipe, full iPad photo/label coverage, and contrast review remain open. |

The candidate does **not** connect to Dexcom or Libre, compute insulin units, provide a correction or treatment recommendation, measure portions from an image, or reliably recognize arbitrary home-cooked, restaurant, mixed, and culturally varied meals. Those missing capabilities must not be implied by the demo. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and the [competitive review](COMPETITIVE-REVIEW.md) for the product direction and RxFood comparison.
