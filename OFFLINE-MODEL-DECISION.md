# Offline meal photo model decision — October 3, 2026

The Challenge candidate must stay fully offline and below the size limit in Apple's currently published [2026 terms](https://developer.apple.com/swift-student-challenge/policy/). A food name from a photo is a clue to confirm, never a source for recipe, portion, carbohydrate grams, or insulin units. We will not ship a model solely because it recognizes the three bundled examples.

| Option | What the source establishes | Decision now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Vision classification | The installed iOS 26.5 Simulator produced the same nonfood labels for three distinct meal photos, while macOS Vision recognized the biryani. An isolated probe of Apple's newer Swift [`ClassifyImageRequest`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/classifyimagerequest) reproduced the same labels on its CPU path and once failed to create an Espresso context on its default path. On a separate 200-photo UECFoodPix meal sample, the Mac Vision path offered at least one mapped clue for 91 photos but made plainly incompatible suggestions, including Quinoa for potato salad. Four overlapping crops raised union clue coverage to 120 but added incompatible clues and two flower false positives. The [protocol and result](research/vision/README.md) are research only. The app reports the known simulator result as unavailable and keeps all other photo clues excluded until review. | Keep the honest fallback; switching Vision APIs or adding crops does not fix the measured trust gap. Obtain physical iPhone/iPad evidence and a stronger independently evaluated model before treating photo names as reliable. |
| Apple's Foundation Models image input | [Apple documents image attachments](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/analyzing-images-with-multimodal-prompting), but the installed Xcode 26.6 SDK has no usable image attachment API. [Xcode 27 requires macOS 26.6 or newer](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/system-requirements/); this Mac is on 26.4. Apple Intelligence availability also varies by device and region. | Reassess with a compatible toolchain and hardware. It cannot be the sole path for an all-ages offline experience on supported older devices. |
| Apple MobileCLIP research weights | Apple's [model license](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-mobileclip/blob/main/LICENSE_MODELS) limits weights and derivatives to research purposes and expressly excludes product development. | Do not bundle these weights in MealMirror. The repository code license is a separate matter. |
| Microsoft TinyCLIP ViT-8M/16 + Text-3M | The [model card](https://huggingface.co/wkcn/TinyCLIP-ViT-8M-16-Text-3M-YFCC15M) marks it MIT and reports 41.1% ImageNet zero-shot accuracy; the [source license](https://github.com/microsoft/Cream/blob/main/TinyCLIP/LICENSE) is MIT. The published checkpoint is 93,812,468 bytes. Our 8-bit Core ML image encoder is 8,505,885 bytes; a hypothetical ZIP containing it is 20,515,479 bytes. On 505 Food-101 photos spanning 101 classes, it ranked the correct class first 292 times. A strict exploratory cutoff kept 69 photos. With only food prompts, that same cutoff mislabeled one flower and four pets as food. Adding hand-chosen nonfood prompts removed those errors on the same negatives. An isolated iOS Simulator probe loaded the compiled encoder and returned a 512-value result. On the independent 1,000-photo [UECFoodPix](https://mm.cs.uec.ac.jp/uecfoodpix/) test split, its top name agreed with a visible labeled category 303 times; the prechosen strict cutoff retained 40 names, of which 34 agreed and 6 did not. | Size and Simulator execution are feasible, but the cross-dataset precision and coverage are too weak for a trusted suggestion path. Keep it out of the app. See [reproducible experiment](research/tinyclip/README.md). |
| Microsoft TinyCLIP auto ViT-22M/32 + Text-10M | The [official model zoo](https://github.com/microsoft/Cream/tree/main/TinyCLIP) reports 53.7% ImageNet zero-shot accuracy and links the checkpoint. The 114,214,705-byte checkpoint was loaded with `weights_only=True` from SHA-256 `fadfe0486c7eb64208d2cfe4dec08120b284a37a11dc2c63cb5dfbac0ed4f018`. On the same 1,000 UECFoodPix photos and 100 dish/20 reject prompts, its top name agreed with a visible labeled category 387 times. At a 0.05 score gap, it kept 64 food names; 60 agreed and 4 did not. The previously hand-chosen reject prompts produced zero food tops on 102 flower and 111 pet photos, which is not independent negative validation. No Core ML conversion, final ZIP sizing, or physical-device run was performed. | Better than the 8M candidate on this dish benchmark, but still not broad or reliable enough for the any-meal path. Do not bundle it; evaluate stronger and culturally varied options with independent abstention calibration. See [research protocol](research/tinyclip/README.md). |
| Food-101 MobileNetV3-Large | An [upstream compact model](https://github.com/yueyang-yin/food-101) reports 82.76% on its own Food-101 test set and publishes 17.5 MB weights plus a 13.6 MB selective INT8 ONNX artifact. Our local check on 200 varied UECFoodPix photos found only 17 exact top-name agreements with a visible mask category; different taxonomies make that a narrow proxy. A top probability ≥0.5 still named food for 38/100 flower photos and 7/100 pet photos. See the [reproducible screen](research/food101-mobilenet/README.md). | Do not integrate. Food-101 accuracy does not establish mixed-meal or nonfood reliability, and no Core ML/device/package integration has been validated. |

The UECFoodPix benchmark is research-only; its provider permits non-commercial research and its images are not redistributed. Its Japanese and mixed-dish categories improve the evaluation breadth, but they still do not cover meals from everywhere. The next model experiment needs a lawful set of real, diverse home, school, restaurant, mixed-dish, packaged, and nonfood photos, with consent and known labels. Measure top suggestions, abstention, false food matches, latency, device support, and final ZIP size. Keep a manual path for every meal. Until that evidence exists, the app must say when photo inspection is unavailable or uncertain and let the person use a trusted carbohydrate source.
