This is public-page research, not a signed-in walkthrough, an accuracy comparison or a clinical assessment. Four official/public primary pages were checked. Other competitors in the older comparison were not re-audited. No third-party account, proprietary code, asset or private API was accessed.

## Review sample and limits

Four unique entries appeared on one listing: useful-information praise (“1d ago”), access friction (June 11, 2024), nutrition-context praise (October 1) and search/slowness criticism (October 15). October years and reliable stars were not exposed. One excerpt: “search feature barely works, takes forever to respond”. Fix status is unknown. The earlier favourites report was not redisplayed.

This is below the skill's broad research target. No trend, failure rate, weighted sentiment or superiority claim follows; no review is used as a testimonial.

## Ranked delivery priorities

1. **Complete the actual offline photo journey on the current candidate.** Verify photo(s) plus a separate label, a tentative clue or honest unknown, correction, separately checked amount, confirmation, saved provenance and relaunch/delete. Test model unavailability too. Build 13 has one complete exposed case; build 16 has not completed the fresh full case. Physical camera/airplane-mode testing remains applicant-deferred.
2. **Make interruption and correction reliable.** Preserve the first failed test and check the real UI contract before editing the app. The current frontend batch still needs a terminal result, visible frames and the rest of its journey. Actual accessibility text sizes and Reduce Motion must be read back and exercised.
3. **Evaluate real recognition with qualified labels.** Varied cuisine, mixed/covered dishes, drinks and nonfood inputs need independent labels and fixed evaluation criteria. Do not tune on reserved examples or convert names into inferred nutrition/doses.
4. **Retain low-burden access and data integrity.** No required account or network belongs in the core local experience. Build 15 protects malformed saved review identities. Timestamp editing, reusable meals and optional export remain broader roadmap candidates, requiring their own design/provenance/privacy checks; they are not silently substituted for the photo goal.
5. **Keep connected services separate and truthful.** Dexcom/Libre access is vendor-gated and network-dependent; offline cached values must show age/source if implemented. Live integration is not currently available in the Challenge candidate. No dose output without individualized clinical validation.
