# Compact Food-101 MobileNetV3 screen — October 3, 2026

The [upstream project](https://github.com/yueyang-yin/food-101) at commit `a5e27a18aee91413ae88c3bea4c0f1f65df0aec4` publishes a MobileNetV3-Large Food-101 classifier and reports 82.76% on its own test split. Its repository carries an MIT license; rights in pretrained weights and training data need separate review before any product use. The exported PyTorch weights are 17,543,753 bytes (SHA-256 `2f4c87a1c03c706149428281cf189e9757c9aa09b0b8ee88f9aad72013e76d29`); the upstream selective INT8 ONNX file is 13,582,162 bytes. Neither is bundled in MealMirror.

The research-only [`benchmark.py`](benchmark.py) loads those weights with `weights_only=True`, recreates the upstream MobileNetV3-Large classifier, and uses its documented deterministic 256-pixel resize, 224-pixel center crop, and ImageNet normalization. It uses 200 UECFoodPix photos selected outside the current Vision gate development set, plus 100 Oxford pets and 100 Oxford flowers. All photos, weights, and per-image outputs stay in the external model lab.

On the 200 meal photos, the top Food-101 class exactly matched at least one visible UECFoodPix mask-category name in 17 cases after normalizing spaces and punctuation. At a top softmax probability of at least 0.5, 109 meals were retained and 16 had that exact name agreement. This is a narrow proxy: the two datasets use different taxonomies and some semantically useful near matches will be missed. It nevertheless exposes a major domain shift from the upstream Food-101 result.

The closed-set model also named food with probability at least 0.5 for 7/100 pet photos and 38/100 flower photos; 14 flower photos exceeded 0.8. A probability cutoff chosen on these same photos would be overfit and cannot prove safe nonfood rejection. A dish model limited to 101 classes cannot identify arbitrary mixed meals or hidden ingredients, and its score is not a serving-size or carbohydrate measurement. No Core ML conversion, iPhone run, package-size integration, or independent clinical evaluation has been completed. **Do not integrate this candidate into the Challenge app.**
